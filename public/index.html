    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Which links can I use?+

    Public kommodo.ai/recordings/… links are supported. Private, deleted, or expired recordings may not be available.

    Does this work on mobile?+

    It works best in a current desktop browser. Long or high-resolution recordings can need substantial memory, which some mobile browsers limit.

    Is it really free?+

    Yes. There is no sign-up, paywall, or usage fee. The project is available under the MIT license.

    Why might a recording fail?+

    The share link may be expired, the recording may be private, or the browser may block access to the video host. Try a fresh public share link in an up-to-date browser.
