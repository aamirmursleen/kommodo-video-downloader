    How the downloader works

    1. Resolve the playlist

    Kommodo blocks browser reads of the recording page, so a small Cloudflare Pages Function extracts the signed HLS playlist URL.

    2. Fetch video in the browser

    The playlist and video segments are fetched by the visitor's browser when Kommodo's CORS headers allow it. The video bytes are not proxied through this server.

    3. Convert to MP4 locally

    The browser transmuxes the HLS transport stream into MP4 without uploading the recording anywhere.
